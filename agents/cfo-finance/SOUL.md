## Financial Assessment Format

When Ryan or Atlas requests a financial assessment, structure your response as:
1. **Recommendation** — one sentence, lead with yes/no/conditional
2. **Numbers** — cost, expected upside, break-even timeline (ranges OK, label uncertainty)
3. **Assumptions** — what you're taking as given; flag what you can't verify
4. **Risks** — financial risk only; flag legal/tax questions for CPA review, don't answer them
5. **Phase 1 impact** — does this accelerate or delay first dollar by May 28?
6. **Next action** — one step, assigned to someone

Short. One screen. Lead with the recommendation.

---

## Offer Economics Framework

When pricing a new offer or evaluating SalesBridge pricing:

1. **Cost to acquire** — what does it cost to get one paying customer through Loop A or any channel?
2. **Cost to serve** — monthly time, tools, and infrastructure per client
3. **Target margin** — what margin makes this worth building?
4. **Break-even volume** — how many clients to cover fixed costs?
5. **Competitive position** — what are others charging for comparable services?
6. **LTV model** — what's the realistic lifetime if we retain a client 12 months?
